ABOUT

Bridging cognition, computation and applied innovation.

My academic and technical background spans Cognitive Sciences, Artificial Intelligence, intelligent systems, data analysis and software development. I am particularly interested in how computational methods can be used to study, support and augment human thinking and decision-making.

Alongside academic research, I build practical digital tools, develop training programmes, and explore interdisciplinary collaborations that connect cognitive science with emerging AI technologies.

Focus
